- [W3C Perspective Videos (2016)](https://www.w3.org/WAI/perspective-videos/) — Short films on why accessibility matters for people with disabilities [English, Captions, Audio Description, Transcript]
- [W3C Stories of Web Users (2025)](https://www.w3.org/WAI/people-use-web/user-stories/) — Stories showing how people with disabilities use the web [English, Captions, Transcript]
- [Handicap Hacks (Pfennigparade)](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL1xCg3ENcWsW32azWVpiqBZdzrdv-gdN8) — People with spasticity, muscular dystrophy, blindness, dysmelia and other conditions show how they use assistive technology such as eye tracking, screen readers, trackballs and voice input to work and spend their leisure time [German]
- [Woche des Sehens](https://www.woche-des-sehens.de/infothek/filme/) — Videos about visual impairment and blindness [German, Audio Description, Subtitles]
- [Barrierefreie Webshops Testvideos (Aktion Mensch)](https://www.aktion-mensch.de/inklusion/studien/test-barrierefreie-webshops/test-videos) — Testers with various disabilities evaluate German online shops for accessibility (keyboard navigation, font size, heading structure) [German]
